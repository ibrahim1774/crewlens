Data deletion

Delete your account and data

How to remove your Crew Lens account and everything in it.

In the app (fastest)

Open Crew Lens, tap You, then Delete account, and confirm. For a company owner this permanently deletes the company and every job, photo, video, document and share link. For a crew member it removes your login and membership; the company keeps the photos you took for its records.

By email

Email support@davoxa.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Crew Lens account". We confirm and delete within 30 days.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you, except records we must keep by law (for example, Apple's purchase records, which Apple holds). Subscriptions are billed by Apple: cancel them in your App Store account settings.

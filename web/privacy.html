Crew Lens privacy

What the app stores, why, and how to get rid of it.

What we collect

Photos and videos you take in the app, the time and GPS coordinates of each one, your name and initials, your company name and trade, and your email or Apple sign-in identifier. Jobs you create, including their address and location.

Why

To file photos to the right job, show your crew and clients what was done and when, and let you share a job by link. Location is used only while the app is open and is stored as a stamp on each photo.

Where it lives

On your device and in our cloud database and storage, hosted by Supabase in the United States. Share links are public to anyone who has the link; revoke a link in the app to stop access.

Analytics

We use PostHog for product analytics and AppsFlyer for install attribution. On iOS we ask for tracking permission first; if you decline, attribution runs without your advertising identifier.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your card details.

Deleting your data

Delete jobs or photos in the app at any time. To delete your account and everything in it, email support@davoxa.com from the address on the account and we will remove it within 30 days.

Contact

Davoxa · support@davoxa.com

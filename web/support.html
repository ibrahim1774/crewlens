Common questions

My photos are not uploading. They save on your phone first and upload by themselves once you have signal. The job shows how many are still uploading.

How do I share a job? Open the job, tap Share, then Live link. The client sees every new photo without a new link.

How do I cancel? In the App Store: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Crew Lens.

How do I delete my account? In the app: You, then Delete account. Or see data deletion.
